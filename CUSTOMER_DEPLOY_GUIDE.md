# 🚀 Quick Start & Deployment Guide: Cyberpunk DevOps Portfolio

Thank you for choosing this Portfolio Template! This portfolio is built with interactive 3D WebGL physics, live CI/CD pipeline simulators, CloudOps command terminals, and a secure Admin CMS.

Follow this simple 3-minute guide to customize your details and launch your website for **100% free**.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Customize Your Details

You can customize your portfolio in **two easy ways**:

### Option A: Edit the Config File (Fastest)
1. Open the file `portfolio-config.json` inside this folder using Notepad, VS Code, or any text editor.
2. Replace the name, role, email, summary, skills, projects, and certifications with your own details.
3. Save the file.

### Option B: Use the Interactive Admin GUI
1. Open the website in your browser (or run locally) and navigate to the hidden URL: `#/admin` (e.g. `http://localhost:5173/#/admin`).
2. Log in with your default Admin passcode: **`Update@123`**.
3. Customize your profile, choose your Cyberpunk Theme, re-order layout sections, and click **Export JSON**.
4. Replace `portfolio-config.json` with your downloaded file.

---

## 🌐 Step 2: Launch Your Website for Free (Pick One)

### 🥇 Method 1: Netlify (Recommended — Fastest Drag & Drop)
1. Go to [Netlify.com](https://www.netlify.com/) and create a free account.
2. In the Netlify dashboard, go to **Sites** and drag and drop this entire folder into the upload box.
3. Your website will be live in **10 seconds** at `https://your-site-name.netlify.app`!
4. *(Optional)* Add your own custom domain for free in Netlify Domain Settings.

---

### 🥈 Method 2: Vercel (1-Click Deployment)
1. Go to [Vercel.com](https://vercel.com/) and sign in with GitHub.
2. Click **Add New** > **Project**, upload your repository, and click **Deploy**.
3. Your website is instantly live at `https://your-name.vercel.app`!

---

### 🥉 Method 3: GitHub Pages
1. Create a new GitHub repository (e.g. `my-portfolio`).
2. Push this folder to your repository.
3. Go to **Repository Settings** > **Pages** > Select `Deploy from a branch` > Choose `main` > `/ (root)` > Click **Save**.
4. Your site will be live at `https://your-username.github.io/my-portfolio`!

---

## 📬 Step 3: Connect Your Contact Form
1. Create a free account at [Formspree.io](https://formspree.io/).
2. Create a new form (e.g. "Portfolio Contact") and copy your **Form ID** (e.g. `mqazkypq`).
3. Open `portfolio-config.json` and replace `"formspreeId": "mqazkypq"` with your own Form ID (or enter it in the Admin Panel).
4. All messages submitted by recruiters will now go straight to your email inbox!

---
